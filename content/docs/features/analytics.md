# Analytics

The Analytics dashboard gives you a real-time view of how visitors are interacting with your AI assistant. Use it to identify knowledge gaps, capture product insights, and improve your assistant over time.

## Accessing Analytics

Go to **Dashboard → Analytics**. The dashboard auto-refreshes and shows data for all your assistants.

---

## Metrics Overview

### Total Conversations

The total number of chat sessions across all your assistants. A session starts when a visitor opens the chat widget and ends when they close it or become inactive.

**Weekly change** is shown as a percentage vs. the previous 7 days.

### Unanswered Questions

Questions your assistant couldn't answer confidently from your knowledge base. These are the highest-priority gaps to fill.

> **Tip:** Export unanswered questions and use them to write new FAQ content — then add that content back as a training source.

### Top User Queries

The most frequently asked questions across all conversations. This shows you:
- What visitors care about most
- Whether your content covers the right topics
- Opportunities for new pages, features, or products

### Top Topics

High-level themes extracted from conversations (e.g. "Pricing", "Integrations", "Returns"). Useful for understanding intent at a glance.

---

## Using Analytics to Improve Your Bot

### Step 1 — Review Unanswered Questions Weekly

Check the **Unanswered Questions** section every week. For each gap:
1. Write the answer
2. Add it as a Plain Text source or update your Notion/Docs content
3. Trigger a sync

### Step 2 — Update Knowledge from Top Queries

If many users ask about a topic that's only partially covered in your knowledge base, expand that content. More detail = better answers.

### Step 3 — Monitor Trends Over Time

A spike in questions about a specific topic often signals:
- A product issue or outage
- A pricing or policy question many users have
- An opportunity for a new FAQ or help article

## Lead Analytics (Professional & Business)

If you have lead capture enabled, the analytics dashboard also shows:

- Total leads captured
- Lead capture rate (leads / conversations)
- List of captured emails and names

Go to **Dashboard → Leads** to view and export the full lead list.
