## Free Trial

Every new account starts with a **7-day free trial** on the Pro plan. You get full access to all Pro features — no credit card needed.

A countdown banner in your dashboard shows how many days remain. At the end of the trial, your account is paused until you select a plan.

---

## Upgrading

1. Go to **Dashboard → Billing**
2. Click **Upgrade Plan**
3. Select your plan and enter payment details
4. Your account is upgraded immediately

Payments are processed securely. We accept all major credit cards.

---

## Downgrading

1. Go to **Dashboard → Billing → Manage Subscription**
2. Select a lower tier
3. The downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing period

> **Note:** Downgrading to Starter removes access to Integrations and Lead Capture. Your connected integrations are paused and can be reactivated when you upgrade again — no data is lost.

---

## Cancelling

1. Go to **Dashboard → Billing → Manage Subscription**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Your account remains active until the end of the paid period

After cancellation, your account moves to a read-only state. No data is deleted — you can resubscribe at any time to restore full access.

## Billing FAQ

**When am I charged?**
On the same date each month, starting from when you first subscribed.

**Can I get an invoice?**
Yes. Go to **Billing → Customer Portal** to download invoices for all past payments.

**Do you offer annual pricing?**
Annual plans with a discount are available — email **ravitej@askyoursite.in** for details.

**Is there a refund policy?**
We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee from first payment. Contact support within 7 days of your first charge.

**Do you offer discounts for startups or non-profits?**
Yes — email **ravitej@askyoursite.in** with proof of status for a custom discount.

**Which plans include Integrations?**
Integrations (Notion, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Drive, HubSpot, Airtable, Slack, Zapier, Calendly) are available on the **Pro** and **Business** plans only.
